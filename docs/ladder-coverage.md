# Ladder data

Ladder presents two distinct sources. They are never pooled into a combined usage or performance metric.

| Source           | Population                                                     | Measures                                                                           |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pokémon Showdown | Supported monthly regulation/format reports and rating cutoffs | Weighted usage, available counts, builds, and teammates                            |
| Champions        | Captured in-game Doubles season/ranking data                   | Usage rank, teammate ranks, marginal build percentages, and available rank history |

Showdown provides BO1/BO3 and All ratings / 1500+ / 1630+ / 1760+ selections for supported reports. These select the source's rating-cutoff reports and preserve its weighting; they do not refilter individual battles.

Champions provides BO1 without a rating split. Ranks are not usage percentages. Capture time does not establish the complete observation window or sample count; unavailable quantities remain unknown.

Neither integrated source supplies competitive team outcomes. Ladder win rates and joint team matchup performance are therefore unavailable. Tournament performance must not be substituted for Ladder outcomes.

Build distributions are separate marginals. A commonly used nature and spread cannot be assumed to occur together. Nature effects color stats only when the source explicitly pairs that nature and spread.

For Champions captures, identical duplicate build values count once. Conflicting values for the same canonical label are omitted with a source-quality note; remaining percentages and ranks are not renormalized or renumbered. Original source evidence is preserved.

Collection failures retain prior publications. The displayed source, period, format, and rating describe the data actually shown after applying filters.

Sources: [Smogon monthly statistics](https://www.smogon.com/stats/) and [MunchStats in-game capture](https://www.munchstats.com/about/).
